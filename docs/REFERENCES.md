## 2 — Bold sculptural typographic layering

[3D Portfolio — Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/shots/27412412-3D-Portfolio-Ai-WebGL-ThreeJS-WordPress)

Borrow: oversized typography, depth from sculptural objects, strong silhouette, editorial attitude. Don't build a literal chess-themed portfolio.

## 3 — Primary aesthetic north star

[3M Minimalist 3D Designer Portfolio — Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/shots/23852527-3M-3D-Designer-Portfolio-Landing-Page-Website)

**Most important reference.** Borrow: large ribbed/parametric organic-architectural sculpture, confident black/white layout, sculptural lighting, spacing, striking scale, and minimal navigation. Our final sculpture should skew more architectural and structured.
